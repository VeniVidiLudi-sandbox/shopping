## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-17
First release: a shared family shopping list, made for phones.
- Everyone signed in sees the same list of colourful, slightly tilted post-it notes.
- Anyone can add an item (e.g. "Milk x2"); each note shows who added it and how long ago.
- A button marks an item as ordered: it fades, is struck through and drops below the
  outstanding items. "Undo" reverses a mis-tap.
- A small post-it favicon for the browser tab.
- Sign-in is through the platform's login service.
